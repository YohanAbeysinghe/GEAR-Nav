# Taller six-scene figure

[Figure on page 7](after_in_paper.png) ·
[submission proof](after/submission/main.pdf) ·
[arXiv proof](after/arxiv/main.pdf)

The figure remains 3.4 inches wide and increases from 2.36 to 2.75 inches
high (about 16%). The three real-world panels restore 70% of the original
photo height rather than the preceding 50% crops. More of the robots and
surroundings is visible; the previous image regions are all retained.
The simulation panels already show their full 16:9 frames and are unchanged.

The image aspect ratios, path control points, failure endpoints, goal
locations, colors, marker sizes, and 7.14 pt minimum type are preserved.
Before-edit figure, builder, and provenance are saved in this folder.
`verification.json` records the dimension comparison and preservation checks.

The paper text, caption, and evaluation tables are unchanged. Both variants
compiled twice with pdfLaTeX; the submission remains eight pages and the
arXiv version including the supplement remains nine. The figure passed visual
inspection on page 7. Existing draft citation and balancing warnings remain.

Local masters and their builder remain under ignored
`figs/realworld_isaacsim/single_column/`. Only the updated paper PDF and the
paper README are committed to the paper repository.
